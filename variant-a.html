The thesis

The hardest data in the world is public — and almost unusable.

The most consequential systems run on the worst software. Legislative records, customs rulings, energy filings, licensing registries, nuclear references — the data is public, authoritative, and locked behind portals built two decades ago for nobody in particular.

NYSGPT exists to close that gap. We don't bolt a chatbot onto a problem. We ingest the primary record, ground every answer in a verifiable source, and rebuild the interface from the data up — so a stressed parent, an importer, a researcher, or a regulator can get a real answer instead of a 40-page PDF.

01Grounded, not generated. Every claim traces to a primary source. Fluency without traceability is a liability.
02The data is the product. We rebuild the interface from the record up — search, structure, and AI as one surface.
03Ship to production. Live products with real users, not decks. Five of eight are running right now.

How we build

THE SAME ARCHITECTURE BEHIND EVERY PRODUCT ABOVE — REUSABLE, GROUNDED, AND SHIPPED.

01 — INGEST

Tame the primary record

We crawl, dedupe, and structure the authoritative source — even when there's no bulk export, no API, or a pagination wall — into clean, queryable Postgres. The hard part nobody else does.

02 — GROUND

Make the AI accountable

Retrieval over the real corpus, structured output, and a visible chain back to the source. The model reasons over primary data, and every answer can be checked.

03 — SHIP

Rebuild the interface

Search, structured exploration, maps, and AI on one surface — designed for the human who actually needs the answer, and pushed to production with real users.
