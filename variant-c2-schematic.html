The signal

// why these systems stay dark

The most consequential systems run on the worst software. Legislative records, customs rulings, energy filings, licensing registries, nuclear references — public, authoritative, and locked behind portals built two decades ago for nobody in particular.

NYSGPT closes that gap. We don't bolt a chatbot onto the problem. We ingest the primary record, ground every answer in a verifiable source, and rebuild the interface from the data up — so the person who needs the answer gets one, not a 40-page PDF. The same three-stage board, re-laid for each domain.
