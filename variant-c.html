The brief

// why these systems stay broken

The most consequential systems run on the worst software. Legislative records, customs rulings, energy filings, licensing registries, nuclear references — the data is public, authoritative, and locked behind portals drafted two decades ago for nobody in particular.

NYSGPT closes that gap. We don't bolt a chatbot onto the problem. We ingest the primary record, ground every answer in a verifiable source, and re-draft the interface from the data up — so the person who needs the answer gets one, not a 40-page PDF.

[N1]Grounded, not generated. Every claim traces to a primary source. Fluency without traceability is a defect.
[N2]The data is the product. Search, structure, and AI re-drafted as one surface from the record up.
[N3]Ship to production. Live products with real users. Five of eight are running now.

Method — assembly

// the same three operations behind every product above

OP 01 / INGEST

Tame the record

We crawl, dedupe, and structure the authoritative source — even with no bulk export, no API, or a pagination wall — into clean, queryable Postgres. The hard part nobody else does.

OP 02 / GROUND

Make AI accountable

Retrieval over the real corpus, structured output, and a visible chain back to the source. The model reasons over primary data — and every answer can be checked.

OP 03 / SHIP

Re-draft the interface

Search, structured exploration, maps, and AI on one surface — drafted for the human who needs the answer, and pushed to production with real users.
