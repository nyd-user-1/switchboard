The Feature

The most consequential systems run on the worst software.

Legislative records, customs rulings, energy filings, licensing registries, nuclear references — the data is public, authoritative, and locked behind portals built two decades ago for nobody in particular.

The most consequential systems run on the worst software. The information exists; it simply can't be reached by the people who need it.

NYSGPT exists to close that gap. We don't bolt a chatbot onto a problem. We ingest the primary record, ground every answer in a verifiable source, and rebuild the interface from the data up.

So a stressed parent, an importer, a researcher, or a regulator gets a real answer — not a 40-page PDF. Each entry in the contents is the same architecture, set for a different domain.

The MastheadHow we build. Three movements, every issue.
i.

Tame the primary record

We crawl, dedupe, and structure the authoritative source — even when there's no bulk export, no API, or a pagination wall — into clean, queryable Postgres. The hard part nobody else does.

ii.

Make the AI accountable

Retrieval over the real corpus, structured output, and a visible chain back to the source. The model reasons over primary data, and every answer can be checked.

iii.

Rebuild the interface

Search, structured exploration, maps, and AI on one surface — designed for the human who actually needs the answer, and pushed to production with real users.
