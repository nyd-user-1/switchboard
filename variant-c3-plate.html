Notes

The most consequential systems run on the worst software. Legislative records, customs rulings, energy filings, licensing registries, nuclear references — public, authoritative, and locked behind portals built two decades ago for nobody in particular.

NYSGPT closes that gap. We don't bolt a chatbot onto the problem. We ingest the primary record, ground every answer in a verifiable source, and rebuild the interface from the data up. Each part below is the same assembly, drawn for a different domain.
