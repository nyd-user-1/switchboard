Thesis

The most consequential systems run on the worst software.

Legislative records, customs rulings, energy filings, licensing registries, nuclear references — the data is public, authoritative, and locked behind portals built two decades ago for nobody in particular. NYSGPT closes that gap. We ingest the primary record, ground every answer in a verifiable source, and rebuild the interface from the data up — so the person who actually needs the answer gets one, not a 40-page PDF.

Ingest. Ground. Ship.

01 / INGEST

Tame the primary record

We crawl, dedupe, and structure the authoritative source — even with no bulk export, no API, or a pagination wall — into clean, queryable Postgres. The hard part nobody else does.

02 / GROUND

Make the AI accountable

Retrieval over the real corpus, structured output, and a visible chain back to the source. The model reasons over primary data — and every answer can be checked.

03 / SHIP

Rebuild the interface

Search, structured exploration, maps, and AI on one surface — designed for the human who needs the answer, and pushed to production with real users.
